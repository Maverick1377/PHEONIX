✦ A birthday wish ✦ 09 · 01 · 2027

Happy Birthday, Pheonix! ♡

Some people enter our lives as classmates, some become friends, and a few become beautiful memories that we carry along without even realizing it.

And then there's you.

From sharing notes and discussing assignments to random conversations, silly jokes, endless teasing and those little moments that somehow become memorable — we've collected quite a few memories along the way.

I don't know whether you realize it, but your presence has added its own little chapter to my college journey.

Remember all those times when you helped me with something, corrected my ideas, suggested a better version, or simply made an ordinary conversation interesting?

Well, today isn't about assignments, exams, deadlines or who's helping whom.

Today is entirely yours!

I hope this new chapter brings you everything you've been working towards — happiness, good health, success, exciting opportunities, beautiful experiences and countless reasons to smile.

May you achieve things that once seemed impossible, travel to places you've always wanted to visit, and become someone your younger self would be proud of.

And please, never lose that playful side of yours. The world already has enough serious people! 😂

One thing I genuinely hope is that no matter how busy life gets, how far our paths take us, or how many new people we meet, we never completely lose this bond.

Because some memories don't need daily conversations to remain special.

“Some melodies are not meant to be sung in front of a crowd; they are meant to be felt in silence.”

Maybe friendships are a little like that too. ♡

Keep smiling, keep shining, keep annoying people (especially me 😁), and keep being the person you are.

Once again, a very Happy Birthday, Pheonix!
